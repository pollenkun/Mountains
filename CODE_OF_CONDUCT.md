# Code of conduct

Mountains contributors and maintainers are expected to treat people with respect across issues, reviews, discussions, and other project spaces.

Disagreement is welcome when it addresses the work. Explain the concern, listen to replies, and make criticism specific and constructive. Respect differences in experience, language, background, and identity.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, unwanted sexual attention, impersonation, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable. Do not use project spaces to pressure people into sharing credentials or personal details.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content, limit participation, or ban repeat or serious offenders. They should explain decisions privately when appropriate, protect reporter privacy, and apply the same standards to themselves.

Use the private contact published by the repository maintainers to report conduct concerns. This preparation snapshot has no configured private contact; a working channel must be established before opening the project to public participation. Do not disclose sensitive reports in public issues.
